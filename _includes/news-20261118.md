Our group has received funding from the Research Council of Finland (RCF) through the RCF-NRF bilateral call! <i class="fa-solid fa-trophy fa-lg"></i> The project is funded with <b>€0.7M</b> in total (Finnish side) over the next 3 years.

<i class="fa-solid fa-microchip fa-lg"></i> <a href="https://research.fi/en/results/funding/89461" target="_blank" class="pub-hover"><b>OSCAR</b>: Open-Source AI-Driven PA-DPD with Computing-In-Memory Acceleration for Energy-Efficient 6G FR3 Wideband Transmitters</a>
