🇰🇷🇫🇮 On September 11 and 12, we hosted the <b><a href="{{ '/assets/Kor-Fin-flyer26.pdf' | relative_url }}" target="_blank">2026 Korea-Finland Chip Design Symposium</a></b> at Aalto University, the second edition following last year's inaugural event!

Supported by the <b>Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Republic of Korea</b>, the symposium once again brought together researchers and industry experts from 🇰🇷 Korea, 🇫🇮 Finland, 🇨🇭 Switzerland, 🇺🇸 United States, and 🇦🇪 UAE, covering integrated circuit design from quantum computing and AI to wireless and sensing systems.

Many thanks to all invited speakers, the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Finland, and our volunteer students! The full program is available on the <a href="/events/">Events</a> page.
