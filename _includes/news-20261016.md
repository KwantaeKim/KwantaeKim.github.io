We open-source <a href="https://github.com/TSircGroup/monarchgru" target="_blank" class="pub-hover"><i class="fa-brands fa-github"></i> MonarchGRU</a>, the PyTorch training framework from our <b><i>IEEE AICAS 2026</i></b> paper presented by <b>Soujanya</b>. <br>

<b>MonarchGRU</b> is a two-layer GRU for low-footprint, always-on keyword spotting. With only <b>29K</b> parameters, it reaches <b>97.94%</b> accuracy on 12-class Google Speech Commands Dataset (GSCD) at about <b>50%</b> lower compute and memory cost than the baseline GRU.

Many thanks again to our collaborators <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/sangyeobkim/" target="_blank">Sangyeob Kim</a>, <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/qinyu" target="_blank">Qinyu Chen</a> and <a href="https://www.tudemi.com" target="_blank">Chang Gao</a>!
